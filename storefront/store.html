Buying Equipment — FAQ

Do you offer financing? +

We can point you to financing options for larger purchases like riding mowers — mention it in the notes field when you submit your order and we'll go over it on the confirmation call.

What's covered under warranty? +

Every unit carries its full manufacturer warranty, and we handle warranty service locally instead of you having to ship anything back.

How far does local delivery reach? +

Delivery covers our service area and the surrounding neighborhoods. If you're outside that range, pickup at our shop is always free.

Can I trade in my old mower? +

Yes — bring it along when you pick up your new equipment, or mention it in your order notes and we'll appraise it ahead of time.
